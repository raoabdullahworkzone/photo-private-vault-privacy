Privacy Policy

Photo Private Vault · Last updated: 21 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Photo Private Vault ("the app", "we", "us") handles your information. We built this app to protect your private photos and videos, and we designed it to keep your data on your device. By using the app you agree to this policy.

The short version

  • Your photos, videos, albums and PIN stay on your device. We cannot see them.
  • Your media is encrypted on your device and we never upload it to our servers.
  • We do not sell your data and the app shows no ads.
  • We collect limited, anonymous diagnostic data to keep the app stable (see below).

Information stored only on your device

The following never leaves your phone and is not accessible to us:

  • Photos & videos you import — stored encrypted inside the app's private storage.
  • Your PIN / passcode — stored as a salted, hashed value (never in plain text).
  • Albums, favourites, trash, and app settings.
  • Break-in photos — if you enable Break-in Alerts, a front-camera photo is taken on a failed unlock attempt and stored encrypted on your device only. It is never uploaded.

Permissions we request and why

  • Photos / Media / Storage — to let you import photos and videos into the vault, and to export them back if you choose. We only access the files you select.
  • Camera — to capture a photo inside the app, and (optionally) for the Break-in Alerts feature. The camera is never used in the background without your action.
  • Internet — used only for anonymous diagnostics/analytics described below.

Diagnostic & analytics data

To keep the app working and fix crashes, we use Google Firebase (Crashlytics and Analytics). This may collect:

  • Crash logs and technical error information.
  • Anonymous usage and device information (e.g. device model, OS version, app version, a non-identifying app-instance identifier).

This data does not include your photos, videos, PIN, or album contents. It is processed by Google under their Firebase Privacy terms.

Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect or upload your photos, videos, or vault contents.
  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, or precise location.
  • We do not use advertising or share data with advertisers.

Data retention & deletion

Because your vault data lives on your device, you control it. Deleting an item moves it to the in-app Trash and it is permanently removed after 30 days (or immediately if you empty the Trash). Uninstalling the app removes all of its on-device data. To delete diagnostic data associated with your app instance, uninstall the app or contact us at the address below.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest version.
